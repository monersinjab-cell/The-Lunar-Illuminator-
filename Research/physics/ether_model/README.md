The Ether Model and the Quranic Research Protocol

Project: "A Luminous Moon" (Qamaran Munira) — Status: Conceptual Model and Prototype

Foundations

Preserving the Quranic text without alteration.

Refraining from adding automated interpretations to the Quranic text.

Establishing a link between the Quranic text, a hypothesis, and empirical measurement to derive conclusions.

A framework for validation based on the existence of a physical ether.

Outcomes, results, and the degree of verification.

Fields of Study

Physics, mathematics, biology, history, linguistics, smart electronics, and data analysis.

Scope of the Model

Simulation and control of physical hardware to validate a physical hypothesis. Any research connection to a Quranic verse does not require explicit textual evidence, and the results of the model are not attributed to the Quran itself.
